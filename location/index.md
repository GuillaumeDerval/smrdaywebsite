The Belgian SMR Day 2027 will be held in **Brussels**. The exact venue is not fixed
yet — we are looking for a location that is easy to reach by train for
participants coming from across Belgium and from neighbouring countries.

The map is centred on Brussels for now and will be updated with the precise
address, along with travel and access information, as soon as the venue is
confirmed.
