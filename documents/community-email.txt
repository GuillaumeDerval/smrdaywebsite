Subject: Join us for Belgian SMR Day 2027

Dear colleagues,

We are pleased to invite you to Belgian SMR Day 2027, a one-day meeting for the
small modular reactor (SMR) research community. Researchers from across
disciplines and institutions will have the opportunity to share their work,
exchange ideas and build new collaborations, with space for dialogue across
the wider SMR ecosystem.

Date: Thursday, 18 March 2027
Location: Brussels, Belgium (venue to be announced)

The programme will include talks, a poster session and a panel discussion.
The poster call will open soon and welcomes contributions across the SMR value
chain, at any stage of work relevant to potential activities in Belgium.

Learn more:
https://smrday.be/
Programme: https://smrday.be/program/
Call for posters: https://smrday.be/call-for-abstracts/

Please share this invitation with colleagues who may be interested.

Best wishes,
The Belgian SMR Day organising team
