## What is the Belgian SMR Day?

Small Modular Reactors are moving quickly from concept to deployment, and in
Belgium the work is spread across universities and research centres, federal and
regional administrations, regulators, utilities and a growing industrial supply
chain. Each of these communities has a clear view of its own piece of the
picture and a much fuzzier view of the others'.

The Belgian SMR Day 2027 is a one-day meeting to fix that. It puts **scientific
researchers, policymakers and industry** in the same room for a day so that
each can present what they are actually working on — results, roadmaps,
constraints, open questions — and so that concrete collaborations can grow out
of those conversations.

The format is deliberately simple: a day of talks and posters, with enough
unstructured time around them for the discussions that matter. Whether you work
on reactor physics, materials, fuel cycle, safety and licensing, siting, grid
integration, economics, public acceptance or regulation, if it touches SMRs in
Belgium, it belongs here.

The day runs from 09:00 to roughly 16:45, with contributed talks in the morning
and late afternoon, a joint overview of SMR research at the Belgian universities
after lunch, an hour-long poster session, and a panel. The
[provisional programme]({{ '/program/' | relative_url }}) is online.
