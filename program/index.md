The programme below is **provisional**. The overall shape of the day is settled
— a morning and late-afternoon block of contributed talks, a joint overview of
SMR research at the Belgian universities after lunch, an hour-long poster
session with the afternoon coffee served alongside it, and a panel — but the
individual talk slots will be filled from the
[call for abstracts]({{ '/call-for-abstracts/' | relative_url }}), and the
panel topic and panellists are still to be announced.
